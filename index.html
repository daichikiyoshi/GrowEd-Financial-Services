By starting at birth, they choose a structured, simple annuity plan that makes saving manageable and organized through regular monthly deposits.

ANNUITY EXPLANATION

Simple Ordinary Annuity

What type of annuity is used?

The brochure describes a simple annuity because the payment period matches the interest-compounding period. Deposits are made monthly and interest is compounded monthly. It is also an ordinary annuity.

Future Value Formula

FV = A × [((1 + i)n − 1) / i]
  • FV: ₱2,000,000
  • Annual interest rate: 4%
  • Monthly rate: 0.04 ÷ 12 = 0.00333333333
  • Number of payments: 18 × 12 = 216
